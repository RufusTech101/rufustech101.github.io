Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Rufus is a private, single-user application. With the owner's explicit authorization through Google sign-in, it accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar data in order to read, organize, and summarize the owner's own information.

Rufus does not sell, share, or transmit any Google user data to third parties. Data is processed on the owner's own devices and stored only within the owner's own accounts and hardware. No data is used for advertising, and no data from anyone other than the owner is collected.

Rufus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access may be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: rufustech101@gmail.com
